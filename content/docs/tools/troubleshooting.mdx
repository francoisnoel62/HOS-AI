This page will help you get unstuck, whether you know the symptom, have an error message or a code. You will be able to find a problem:

- by symptom, in plain words: "the hos command is not found", "everything is `undeclared_capability`", "my program passes on my computer but not in the test", "the signature has expired";
- by the exact message: paste it in the search box;
- by code: the exit codes 0, 1 and 2, and the seven signature errors.

Each entry will give the message, what it means, why it happens, how to fix it on each system, how to check the fix, and where to ask if it still fails.

Meanwhile, the "On Windows" section of the [npm page of the hos command](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hos-ai/cli) covers the most common problems on Windows, and you can [ask for help](/docs/tools/help).
