This guide will build, step by step, an adapter that turns a system's data into HOS facts with the SDK, following the Mews, Apaleo and Cloudbeds mappings:

- keeping HOS ids opaque and stable with an identity registry;
- writing facts with `createFactWriter`: the envelope, the time basis, the actor and the business date;
- the time helpers, and daylight saving time;
- validating facts before publishing them, and checking the producer in tests;
- a runnable example, driven by a made-up housekeeping webhook.

Meanwhile, the [npm page of the SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hos-ai/sdk) has examples of each step.
