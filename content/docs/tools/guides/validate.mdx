This guide will show how to check HOS documents and streams with `hos validate`:

- how the command recognises an event, a situation or a manifest on its own;
- single files (`.json`), streams (`.jsonl` or `.ndjson`), and reading from standard input with `-`;
- `--manifest`, which only applies to streams, and `--json`;
- how to read a line of output: the verdict, the level of each message (`error`, `warning` or `note`), the rule and the path;
- what a stream reveals that a single document cannot: duplicates, the same id with different content, inconsistent tenants or time zones, undeclared facts and competing authorities.
