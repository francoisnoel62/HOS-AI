This guide will take a vendor through signing its producer manifest and publishing it:

- why sign: a manifest that fails verification counts as no manifest, so nothing it declares is processed;
- creating a key with `hos manifest keygen`, and signing with `hos manifest sign`;
- serving the three files, `manifest.json`, `manifest.jws` and `jwks.json`, under `https://<your origin>/.well-known/hos/`, with recipes for common hosts;
- checking the result with `hos manifest verify`, from the files or from the URL, and `--at` to test a future date;
- signing again before the signature expires, and rotating keys;
- keeping the private key safe, and what to do if it leaks;
- producers that are not public.

Meanwhile, [HOS Events 0.1](/docs/events#signed-manifests) states the signing rules, and the [npm page of the hos command](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hos-ai/cli) has the three commands.
